# Join Us

HelSi Lab welcomes students interested in bio/healthcare systems, simulation-based
optimization, digital twins, and data-driven modeling.
학부 연구생 및 대학원생 모집 — 시뮬레이션 · 최적화 · 디지털 트윈에 관심 있는 학생을 찾습니다.

## Who we're looking for

- Students with the curiosity and persistence to learn how research works
- Interest in bio/healthcare systems, simulation-based optimization, or digital twins
- Preferred: completion of the Simulation course, and comfort with programming (Python or similar)

## What you'll get

- Regular research meetings and one-on-one mentoring
- Opportunities to present at domestic and international conferences and to co-author papers
- Vacation-period seminars and journal clubs (this year's theme: agent-based simulation)
- International collaborations with partners in the US

## Undergraduate researchers

- 3rd–4th year students — up to 2 positions
- A 2-month internship period, followed by full membership in the lab
- Monthly stipend

## Graduate students (M.S. / Ph.D.)

- Full tuition support plus a monthly stipend
- Research topics across our three areas: bio/healthcare systems modeling, simulation methodology, and simulation optimization & explainable AI

## How to apply

Email Prof. Serin Lee with a short note about your interests and background.
An informal chat follows, then final selection.
