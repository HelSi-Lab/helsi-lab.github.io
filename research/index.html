HEALTHCARE & PUBLIC HEALTH

Modeling policy,
care, and prevention.

SIMULATION & DIGITAL TWINS

Making complex systems
visible and testable.

OPTIMIZATION & EXPLAINABLE AI

Finding the right
decision under uncertainty.
